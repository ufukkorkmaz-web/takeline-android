Studio Voice

The green noise button turns on offline cleanup of recorded takes. Browser suppression is off by default because stacking it with offline cleanup can sound thin or watery. Strong mode may slightly affect voice quality. Changes also reprocess existing takes.

Non-destructive EQ, low-cut filter, gentle compression and peak protection. Original takes remain available.

Reading support

Follows recognized words live. When the end of the line is recognized, recording stops after a short pause; with Auto next enabled the prompter advances. Requires browser speech recognition. Note: your browser's speech service may send audio to a cloud provider. Turn this off for confidential scripts.
